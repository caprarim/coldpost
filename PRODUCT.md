# Product

## Register

product

## Users
One creator running several TikTok accounts from an Android phone. They plan a handful of short videos per account each day, usually late at night, and want each video to go out at a set gap (for example 30 minutes) on the right account.

## Product Purpose
ColdPost is a free, offline posting planner for TikTok. TikTok does not let personal tools auto post publicly, so ColdPost plans the queue, buzzes the phone when a video is due, and opens TikTok with that video loaded and its caption copied. Success is a queue that runs itself: the creator only switches account, pastes and taps Post.

## Brand Personality
Cold, calm, exact. Few words, big targets, no clutter. It should feel like a tool you trust at 3 AM with one eye open.

## Anti-references
Social media dashboards packed with analytics cards, gradients and tiny text. Generic scheduler SaaS in purple on white. Anything that needs a tutorial.

## Design Principles
1. One job per screen. The next action is always the biggest thing on it.
2. The account is never ambiguous. Every video carries its account's color and handle.
3. Words are a last resort. Icons, times and color carry meaning first.
4. Nothing leaves the phone. No sign in, no servers, no tracking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme. Touch targets at least 44 points. Respect system font scaling. Haptics confirm state changes but never carry meaning alone.
